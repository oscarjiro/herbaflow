Principles

Reviewable, not black‑boxed.

Three commitments the product holds itself to — surfaced in the UI, not buried in release notes.

01 / 03

Every association is traceable.

Each edge in the final network carries its source database, the parameters you used, and the cut‑offs that produced it. Click to expand the provenance trail.

02 / 03

Surface the limits.

Database freshness, coverage gaps, and unautomated steps (looking at you, molecular docking) are shown plainly — not hidden behind a confidence number.

03 / 03

Built for the methods section.

The output is a publishable figure plus a structured log of every decision you made. Reproducibility is a feature, not a checkbox.
